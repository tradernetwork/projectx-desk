# Review by gpt-6, 2026-10-14

## Verdict
Do not enable entries in a real-gateway sim rehearsal yet; read-only discovery can proceed.
Three deciding problems: protection disappears on restart or missed events; multiple tickets can create excess stops; flatten can leave entries working or race late writes.
Fix and reproduce those sequences offline before any order-enabled rehearsal.
The single entry-write path and single-use ticket are useful controls, but do not establish lifecycle safety.
No real gateway was contacted for this review.

## Findings

Confidence verified means observed offline or directly established by code; it does not mean verified at ProjectX. The five named reproductions were executed with the existing scripted test broker, temporary state directories and no credentials. Suggestions below are missing regression tests, not claims that tests were added.

### F1 Protection is lost after ACK restart or missed hub fill  [severity: critical]  [confidence: verified]
- Where: `px_guard.py:161`, `px_guard.py:625`, `px_guard.py:691`, `px_state.py:181`, `px_app.py:389`.
- Failure sequence: Entry ACK persists ACKED. Process dies before fill/stop. Restart constructs empty tickets; ACKED is not pending. Broker fills; REST reseed records the position but emits no guard event. Even a later position event finds no ticket. No stop or watchdog is created. Without restart, missing the fill during a hub interruption has the same result because reseed does not call protection handling. Offline reproduction confirmed restart loses the plan and pending block. SENDING on disk does block subsequent opens, but cannot protect an existing fill.
- Fix: Persist complete protection ownership and recover it before READY. Check all positions against verified working protection on boot/resync and during open exposure. Refuse opens while any recovery is unresolved. Add `test_acked_entry_restart_before_fill_recovers_protection` and `test_resync_rest_position_without_hub_fill_is_protected_or_halted`; assert no READY naked position and no duplicate entry write.

### F2 Two approved entries can produce stops larger than the position  [severity: critical]  [confidence: verified]
- Where: `px_guard.py:440`, `px_guard.py:694`, `px_guard.py:706`, `px_guard.py:710`.
- Failure sequence: Place one micro, receive fill and its size-1 stop. Place another micro in the same contract, permitted by symbol cap 2. Net-position event is size 2. Ticket A resizes its stop to 2; ticket B arms a second stop of 2. Offline reproduction confirmed two tickets each claim protection for the full size-2 position. Combined stop quantity is 4. If both independent stops execute, they can create a short position nobody approved.
- Fix: One protection owner per account/contract net position, with explicit scale-in allocation and aggregate stop invariant. Until implemented, refuse a second entry in a nonflat contract and reserve outstanding entry quantities. Add `test_two_tickets_same_contract_have_total_stop_qty_equal_net` and `test_two_pending_limit_entries_reserve_symbol_headroom`. Assert total independently executable exit quantity cannot exceed net and working entries cannot oversubscribe caps. Do not implement reservation by blocking emergency exits behind the entry lock.

### F3 Resize widens a stop that the trader already tightened  [severity: high]  [confidence: verified]
- Where: `px_guard.py:757`, `px_guard.py:762`, `px_guard.py:932`.
- Failure sequence: Initial long stop is 4994. Trader tightens to 4998; `stop_current` updates. Position grows. `_resize_stop` modifies size and sends original `stop_plan.stop_price`, 4994. Offline reproduction confirmed the widening. Local `stop_current` still reports 4998, concealing the actual looser stop.
- Fix: Resize at the current verified stop price and serialize resize with move operations using the same protection owner. Add `test_resize_after_tighten_preserves_tightest_stop` plus concurrent move/resize variants; assert broker price and local price agree and never retreat.

### F4 Flatten can create new exposure or leave an entry live  [severity: critical]  [confidence: verified]
- Where: `px_guard.py:809`, `px_guard.py:814`, `px_guard.py:819`, `px_guard.py:835`, `px_guard.py:843`.
- Failure sequence: With no position and a resting entry, FLATTEN returns flat before querying/cancelling orders. Offline reproduction confirmed the entry remains working. It can fill afterward. With a position, a timed-out close can execute after the final REST read but before the fallback opposite market order; that order then opens the opposite position. The latter is a code-supported timing risk, not a real-gateway observation. Cancel response `success:false` is ignored, and sim orphan count stays 0 even after failed cancellation.
- Fix: Treat flat as both zero positions and zero executable entry/exit orders, verified after cancellation. Persist and reconcile ambiguous reducing writes; require broker-verified reduce-only/idempotent semantics for an automatic fallback, otherwise halt and report uncertain exposure for manual broker recovery. Removing automatic fallback adds the explicit risk of leaving an open position longer during an outage. Add `test_flatten_flat_with_working_entry_cancels_and_verifies`, `test_failed_cancel_is_reported_as_orphan`, and `test_late_close_does_not_turn_fallback_into_new_position`.

### F5 A stop arriving after watchdog flatten can become an orphan  [severity: high]  [confidence: likely]
- Where: `px_guard.py:726`, `px_guard.py:733`, `px_guard.py:748`, `px_guard.py:772`, `px_guard.py:777`.
- Failure sequence: Stop POST remains in flight for 5 s. Watchdog flattens and halts; open-order search cannot see the delayed stop yet. Broker later creates it and returns ACK. `_arm_stop` records ACKED and cancels watchdog, with no flat-position orphan cleanup. The independent stop can later open opposite exposure while halted. Separately, watchdog only acts for PLACED, even though fills are deliberately processed in PLACING and UNKNOWN. If entry reply is still pending or ambiguous, a hung stop gets no watchdog recovery at expiry.
- Fix: Protection workflow must track late completion after flatten and cancel/verify any late stop on sim. Persist uncertainty and keep unresolved state on failed cleanup. Watchdog should depend on actual exposure/protection state, not entry ACK state. Add `test_late_stop_ack_after_watchdog_flatten_is_cancelled`, `test_stop_timeout_while_entry_placing_flattens`, and `test_stop_timeout_after_unknown_entry_flattens` using independently delayed entry/stop replies.

### F6 HALT can arrive during freshness reads and still allow an entry  [severity: high]  [confidence: verified]
- Where: `px_guard.py:355`, `px_guard.py:575`, `px_guard.py:587`, `px_guard.py:594`.
- Failure sequence: Evaluation checks halt, quote age, hub and day rules. PLACE awaits positions/orders. MCP HALT or UI HALT writes the file during that await. Matching REST data returns; PLACE sends without rechecking halt. Offline reproduction confirmed an accepted entry with the halt file already present. Long limiter waits can similarly invalidate quote freshness, cutoff and ticket TTL. A read spanning 17:00 CT can validate against the old day's P&L while counting the order on the new day.
- Fix: Recheck non-I/O gates and state/version after awaited reads, immediately before committing the intent. Define HALT linearization relative to a committed write; a committed POST cannot be recalled. Add `test_halt_during_fresh_state_read_prevents_entry`, `test_quote_expires_during_rest_read_prevents_entry`, `test_ticket_expires_during_rest_read_prevents_entry`, and `test_trade_date_roll_during_place_revalidates_budget`.

### F7 Read errors can be mistaken for an empty broker book  [severity: high]  [confidence: verified]
- Where: `px_client.py:168`, `px_client.py:172`, `px_client.py:176`, `px_client.py:182`; `px_guard.py:789`.
- Failure sequence: HTTP 200 contains `success:false` and omits positions/orders/trades. Wrapper returns `[]` without checking success. Boot can seed empty state and known zero P&L; flatten can declare flat; reconcile can report NOT_FOUND. These read wrappers unlike bars do not validate broker success. The consequence depends on actual gateway failure payloads, which I did not verify.
- Fix: Validate success, expected list schema and required row fields for all safety reads; distinguish an authenticated successful empty list from missing data. Add `test_unsuccessful_position_search_is_unknown_not_flat`, `test_unsuccessful_trade_search_does_not_seed_zero_pnl`, and `test_missing_orders_list_does_not_resolve_intent` with HTTP 200 failure envelopes.

### F8 Rejection with an order id disables protection despite documented ambiguity  [severity: high]  [confidence: verified]
- Where: `px_guard.py:628`, `px_guard.py:697`; `tests/test_px_guard_place.py:test_broker_rejection_keeps_order_id_and_does_not_halt`.
- Failure sequence: Gateway replies `success:false` plus an order id. Code explicitly comments that an order may still exist, then marks REJECTED and allows new entries. A subsequent position event is ignored because REJECTED is excluded from protection handling. The user-provided ProjectX facts specifically warn that wrong bracket mode can reject while creating an order. Current test pins no halt but does not simulate subsequent exposure.
- Fix: Reconcile existence/executability when rejection includes an id or comes from an ambiguous bracket outcome; continue protecting matching exposure until proven safe. Add `test_rejected_entry_with_order_id_then_fill_is_protected_and_halted`. Distinguish terminal rejected history rows from working/filled orders by verified broker status, not existence alone.

### F9 The advertised budget and discipline rails are incomplete  [severity: high]  [confidence: verified]
- Where: `px_guard.py:274`, `px_guard.py:335`, `px_guard.py:427`; `px_limits.py:163`; `px_state.py:211`, `px_state.py:242`; `README.md:95`.
- Failure sequence: Static drawdown room is reused despite unrealized loss and existing stop risk. Multiple new entries each spend the same room. A rejected subsequent entry still replaces `last_risk` before sending; the next losing fill's size lock reads that most recent attempted order, not the losing trade's original risk. External trades have no known risk cap and are allowed after cooldown. Missing state files reset count/risk; duplicate trade hub events append twice and may inflate wins or loss streaks. No broker calendar/deadline enforcement closes exposure at cutoff.
- Fix: Reserve aggregate stop risk plus fees/slippage, use verified firm floor/equity, and associate initial risk with completed position cycles. Unknown loss risk should refuse discretionary size increases until an operator resolves it. Deduplicate trade ids and validate day-state schema. Add `test_open_stop_risk_reduces_next_entry_budget`, `test_rejected_entry_does_not_replace_losing_trade_risk`, `test_duplicate_fill_id_does_not_change_pnl_or_streak`, and `test_missing_state_after_prior_run_blocks_until_recovery`. Add deadline tests for holiday early close, restart near cutoff and backward wall-clock jump. Stops bound intended risk, not maximum realized loss under slippage.

### F10 Some AI tools still forward untyped gateway values  [severity: medium]  [confidence: verified]
- Where: `px_mcp.py:111`, `px_mcp.py:127`, `px_mcp.py:136`; `px_app.py:133`, `px_app.py:210`, `px_app.py:363`.
- Failure sequence: Hostile normal contract description/tag/error text is removed by inspected paths. However MCP quote returns raw last/bid/ask; bars return raw timestamp/OHLCV; contract search returns raw tick values. A gateway string in a numeric field reaches the model unchanged. `/contracts` also forwards unvalidated id and derives fallback name from it; `/state.json` forwards raw quote fields including timestamp. Thus field-name allowlisting is not a type allowlist.
- Fix: Apply finite numeric validation and parsed timestamps at every output boundary; validate ids before deriving names. Add `test_mcp_numeric_fields_reject_gateway_instruction_strings`, `test_contracts_invalid_id_cannot_populate_fallback_name`, and `test_state_quote_timestamp_is_parsed_not_forwarded`. Validate any future GEX provider separately: `px_digest.py:341` inserts its dictionary directly.

### F11 Non-loopback startup refusal depends on using main()  [severity: medium]  [confidence: verified]
- Where: `px_app.py:45`, `px_app.py:50`, `px_app.py:449`, `px_net.py:58`.
- Failure sequence: Documented `python px_app.py` validates bind configuration. Alternate ASGI invocation that calls `create_app` and tells uvicorn to bind publicly bypasses `main()`; absent operator token means login middleware allows requests. A non-browser caller can supply an allowed loopback Host. This is a deployment footgun, not a bypass of a properly configured operator token.
- Fix: Provide one validated launcher and reject missing operator auth in production application configuration, independently of server invocation; pin container/systemd invocation. Add `test_production_app_factory_refuses_missing_operator_token`. Keep TLS/tailnet ACLs and durable volume validation explicit. Full operator credentials deliberately grant order authority, so protect their distribution separately from MCP credentials.

## Answers to the 8 questions
1. **Order safety:** F1 to F8 provide exact sequences. I found no same-ticket entry replay inside one Guard instance; the concurrency test pins that. I did find extra stop quantity, an entry after HALT, and several ways to create unapproved opposite exposure. Halted reducing actions are intentional; the defect is that some can create exposure. One-process locks do not cover two desk processes sharing a broker account or state directory.
2. **UNKNOWN:** Never replay remains correct even if tags fail. Tags are evidence for investigation, not proven idempotency. Rehearsal order: verify account simulation/bracket configuration and position side mapping; verify native bracket acceptance and rejection-with-id behavior at one micro; verify actual position/order/trade events and reconnect snapshots; verify generated tag length, exact echo in open and historical searches, terminal statuses and visibility delay; deliberately delay a successful POST reply and observe whether it lands after client timeout; verify restart recovery, orphan cleanup and flatten outcomes. Do not infer nonexecution from one negative tag search. Current reconcile merely changes UNKNOWN to FOUND/PLACED, including a historical cancelled/rejected hit; it does not prove protection or flatness. Do failure injection against a controlled proxy/mock first, then only supervised sim orders with broker UI recovery available.
3. **Prop-firm rules:** A static user-entered figure is defensible only as a manually maintained estimate. It is not a conservative enforcement model while it omits open risk, unrealized loss and fees/slippage reserves. Topstep's published MLL updates at end of day but is enforced intraday on realized and unrealized P&L, so the blueprint's assumed intraday peak trailing would be stricter than that particular model, not an accurate universal rule. [Topstep MLL](https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/8284204-what-is-the-maximum-loss-limit). Flat-by is 3:10 PM CT with earlier product closures possible, rather than a generic CME 17:00 date roll. [Trading hours](https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/8284206-when-and-what-products-can-i-trade). Consistency depends on account path and profit-day aggregation; a per-trade risk cap cannot enforce it. [Consistency rules](https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/8284208-consistency-at-topstep). Version rules by firm/account program; do not ship guessed thresholds for an unchosen firm.
4. **AI surface:** Normal names/tags/error messages are well constrained in inspected MCP and digest paths; F10 shows remaining typed-data holes. MCP-only agents cannot invoke place/flatten/cancel/clear. They can supersede human staged tickets, grow the unbounded ticket/history dictionaries, exhaust four persistent quote pins with unwanted contracts, consume REST quotas and halt the desk. Read tools expose configured risk and P&L by design, although no account id/balance is intentionally returned. Stream mode is per request, not a privacy permission. Rate-limit staging and subscriptions, bound retention, and separate read/stage/halt scopes. A browser/shell-capable agent can still use the operator HTTP path; the README acknowledges that boundary.
5. **Sizing and rails:** Keep decimal floor, granularity refusal, hard caps, cooldown and an explicit session loss lockout. Loss-linked sizing restraint is valuable once attributed to the actual losing position. ADX/ATR cuts are untested strategy filters; lower exposure is not evidence of better expectancy. At cap 1, they mainly change whether an entry is refused. Scratch fills with fees and duplicate fills need clearer accounting. The single most valuable missing rail is a restart-safe aggregate exposure/protection invariant: every executable entry and open position must have reserved risk and exactly accountable protection. A firm deadline rail comes next. Do not let recommendations to tighten stops merely to fit granularity substitute for a valid trade setup.
6. **Network exposure:** The weakest intended link is custody of a full-authority operator token, particularly over HTTP or a proxy that leaves the loopback backend effectively public. Before container/LXC deployment, use HTTPS via tailnet serve/proxy, restrict direct backend access and tailnet ACLs, enforce authenticated startup across supported launchers, and prove state-volume persistence across container recreation. Separate MCP token is useful but lacks fine-grained staging/halt scopes. Host and Origin checks are browser defenses, not authentication for arbitrary clients. I did not run Docker/LXC/Tailscale.
7. **Docs:** The cold start and glossary make the mock understandable, but a trader still needs a single practical explanation of what to type for daily loss/drawdown room, what updates it, and which broker-side protections to keep enabled. README headline remaining-loss-room enforcement is stronger than actual static/realized accounting. “Stops only tighten” is contradicted by F3. D1 is marked built despite sequential readback/hold rather than concurrent. I2 DONE lists HWM that DayState does not store. D6 says fractions are config but from_env does not read them. “A stop is required” should lead with its event/restart dependency. The LIMITATIONS page is unusually explicit, but those disclosures do not repair lifecycle defects. Separate simulated broker flag from evaluation/funded program status; one does not establish the other.
8. **Product:** Traders will keep a tool that shows one trustworthy remaining-risk figure, refuses oversize entries with a clear alternative, exposes protection status and helps them survive a bad session. Make recovery and firm deadline status first-class. Keep the size box, cooldown, loss lockout, stop management and simple payoff statistics. Collapse level reactions and remove prominent ABOVE_RANDOM verdicts until validated: fixed shifted lines are neither randomized nor independent and the 15-point gap is not a significance test (`px_levels.py:128`, `px_levels.py:147`). Defer GEX provider work, elaborate voice confirmation and broader broker support until the lifecycle and supervised sim evidence are reliable.
