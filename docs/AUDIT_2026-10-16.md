# Full audit, 2026-10-16

A read-only audit of the whole desk, front end and back end, run as one workflow: 15 finders (one per area, two of them driving an
isolated mock desk and the UI), every finding then checked by two independent verifiers (one tries to reproduce it, one tries to refute
it), then deduplicated. It used 192 agents and about 15 million tokens, which was far more than expected, so treat it as a one-off.
88 candidate findings, 77 confirmed, merged into the 26 items below. Many severities were corrected downward: most impact is availability
or display, the guard still refuses, and the precondition is a misconfiguration.

**Themes:** fail-closed rules are correct in code but under-protected by tests (17 of 34 hand mutations survived); one-shot recovery paths
(boot, reseed, CSRF fetch, tick stream) stay degraded after a single transient failure; stream-mode redaction has gaps; some advisory
displays can disagree with the guard; the order-path pin is a tripwire, not a runtime gate.

Only small, reliability-improving items were fixed. The rest is a backlog, not a to-do list: build one only when it is worth its complexity.
